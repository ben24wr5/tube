TUBE MAC PLAYER — macOS app (macOS 13 Ventura or newer)

HOW TO GET THE APP
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Right-click "Build Tube Mac Player.command" and choose Open (then Open again).
3. Wait for it to finish. Tube Mac Player opens and is placed in Applications.

If your Mac has never built an app before, macOS will offer to install Apple's
free command line tools once. Click Install, then run step 2 again.

APP ICON
Your gem logo is already set as the app icon (AppIcon.png) and appears next to the
app name inside the app (Logo.png). To change it, replace those files and build again.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Real YouTube videos: trending feed, search, live, channels and comments
  (paste a free YouTube API key in Settings — steps are shown there)
• Nothing to buy, every feature free (YouTube's own player may show ads on YouTube videos)
• YouTube-style player: click to pause, double-click for full screen, seek bar with
  buffering and hover time, ±10s, volume, speed (0.25x–2x), quality (Auto–2160p),
  autoplay with "Up next" countdown, resume where you left off, mini player
• Picture-in-picture (YouTube videos use a floating pop-out window)
• Downloads with a quality picker for your own uploads and the starter videos
  (YouTube doesn't allow downloading its videos)
• Downloads list (Original, 4K, 1080p, 720p, 540p, 480p, audio only),
  saved to Downloads ▸ Tube Mac Player and playable offline
• Home feed with categories, search, channel pages
• Upload your own videos, comments, likes/dislikes, free subscriptions + bell,
  watch later, history, liked videos
• Live: watch live streams, or go live with your camera (recorded and posted to
  your channel when you end; add an RTMP server in Settings to broadcast)
• Settings ▸ Background theme: Midnight, Daylight, Ocean, Sunset, Lavender,
  Emerald, Diamond, Gold, Silver, Black & Red, Red & Gold

KEYBOARD (on the watch page)
Space/K play-pause • J/L back/forward 10s • ←/→ 5s • ↑/↓ volume • M mute
F full screen • I picture-in-picture • 0–9 jump • Shift+> / Shift+< speed

NOTE
Uploads, comments, likes and subscriptions are stored on this Mac. Sharing them
with other people needs an online server — that's the next step.
